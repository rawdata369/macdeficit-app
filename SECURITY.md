# Security

Mac Deficit reads your whole drive and can move what you choose to the Trash, so a flaw in it matters. If you find one, please tell us.

## Reporting a problem
Write to **support@macdeficit.com** with "Security" in the subject. Please do not open a public issue or discussion for it.

It helps to include:
- the version of Mac Deficit (Mac Deficit › About) and of macOS
- what you did, what happened, and what you expected
- whether it was the direct download or the Mac App Store build

Leave out anything private: a report does not need your file names or their contents.

## What to expect
- A reply within 5 working days saying we have it
- An account of what we found and what we will do, once we have looked
- A fix in an update for anything confirmed, and your name in its notes if you would like

Mac Deficit is made by one person at RawData Studio. There is no bug bounty.

## In scope
- The app deleting, trashing or offering for deletion something it should not: macOS's own files, places macOS protects for your privacy, or anything other than what the confirmation named
- The app sending anything off your Mac. Its one use of the network is Check for Updates…, only when you choose it, which reads a version number from macdeficit.com and sends nothing about you, your Mac or your files
- The app reading or keeping more than it says: its log, its crash reports and its history of actions stay on your Mac
- The download itself: its signature, its notarization, or a copy served from macdeficit.com that is not ours
- The site macdeficit.com

## Out of scope
- What needs someone already in control of your Mac or your account
- macOS's own behaviour, such as what Full Disk Access allows any app that holds it
- Copies of Mac Deficit from anywhere but macdeficit.com or the Mac App Store

## Supported versions
The newest release. Mac Deficit has no release yet; this page stands ready for the first.
